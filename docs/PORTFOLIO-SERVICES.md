# Portfolio services

Source: `gabley`. Placement: `assets` in `src/components/dashboards/LandlordDashboard.tsx`.

Three contextual offers expand to a larger service list. Self, owned and dismissed services are suppressed. Market-specific offers are gated by country. This panel does not activate products, send customer data, process payments, or bind insurance. Enquiry drafts stay on the device.

The shared catalogue is configuration, not proof that every product is live. Existing release gates and hosted acceptance remain required.
