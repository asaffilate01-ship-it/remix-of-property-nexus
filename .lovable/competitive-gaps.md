# Competitive Gap Analysis — Estately

Benchmarked against Arthur Online, Rightmove, Bayut, Reapit/Dezrez, OpenRent, Goodlord.

## P0 — Shipping this pass (UI shells + seeded data)

### Property management (Arthur)
- **Inspections** `/inspections` — schedule + condition reports, photos, action items
- **Owner statements** `/statements` — monthly landlord remittance, fees, P&L
- **Documents vault** *(rolled into Property drawer + Compliance — already present)*

### Agency CRM (Reapit / Dezrez)
- **Viewings calendar** `/viewings` — diary, confirmations, feedback capture
- **Offers ladder & chain tracker** `/offers` — bidding history + sales chain
- **Reports & KPIs** `/reports` — branch performance, vendor reports, stock turn

### Portal (Rightmove / Bayut / OpenRent)
- **Instant valuation** `/valuation` — postcode + beds → AVM estimate, lead capture
- **Mortgage calculator** `/mortgage` — repayment + affordability
- **Area guides** `/area-guides` — schools, transport, demographics index
